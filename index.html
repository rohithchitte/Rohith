What's actually inside

Four herbs, nothing hiding behind them

No artificial flavour, no added colour. Ruby Calm Tea gets its taste and colour from these four herbs alone.

Hibiscus

Gives the tea its deep ruby colour and a naturally tart, refreshing taste. Traditionally valued for its antioxidants and for supporting healthy digestion.

Beetroot

Adds a subtle earthy sweetness and rich colour. Naturally rich in iron and folate, and known for supporting stamina and healthy blood flow.

Moringa

Brings a light, green, earthy note to the blend. Packed with vitamins A and C, calcium, and plant protein — a traditional pick for everyday energy.

Mulethi (Licorice Root)

Rounds off the blend with a gentle natural sweetness. Traditionally used to soothe digestion and calm the throat.
